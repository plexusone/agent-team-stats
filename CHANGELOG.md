## [v0.10.0] - 2026-08-09 ([`34b66c3`](https://github.com/plexusone/agent-team-stats/commit/34b66c3))

### Highlights

- Eino orchestrator rewritten around REAL (discovery) and VEAL (verification) loops, replacing the old linear graph and its unimplemented "Retry Research" step
- `Statistic`/`CandidateStatistic` gain `Precision` and `AsOfDate`, sourced from the LLM and propagated into `claims.StatisticalDetail`
- Known stats-aggregator domains are rejected during verification via `models.IsKnownAggregatorURL`
- Fixed a previously-silent bug where discovery decoded into an unused `candidates` field instead of the research worker's actual `search_results`

### Added

- REAL discovery loop and VEAL verification loop in the Eino orchestrator (`pkg/orchestration/eino.go`) ([`34b66c3`](https://github.com/plexusone/agent-team-stats/commit/34b66c3))

- `models.IsKnownAggregatorURL` and aggregator-aware `classifySourceType`, wired into `claim.SetStatistical(...)` ([`2779a7d`](https://github.com/plexusone/agent-team-stats/commit/2779a7d))
- Research/synthesis LLM prompts now request `precision` and `as_of_date` per statistic ([`d74772e`](https://github.com/plexusone/agent-team-stats/commit/d74772e))

### Fixed

- `ResearchResponse` decoded into an unused `candidates` field instead of the research worker's actual `search_results`, likely causing zero discovery results in production ([`34b66c3`](https://github.com/plexusone/agent-team-stats/commit/34b66c3))
